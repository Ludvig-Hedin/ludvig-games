How to play

01

Make some noise.

Click the colourful pads or use their letter keys. The synth notes fit together automatically. Imported samples keep their own tuning.

02

Catch a good idea.

Press Record and play. Your hits snap into the loop. Or paint the little squares directly. Shift-click gives a stronger accent.

03

Make it yours.

Record your voice or import audio. Mute rows, remix a groove, and queue scenes with keys 1–4.

04

Play the beat.

Beat quest sends notes toward the hit line. Press their keys at the line. Build a streak and beat your local high score.

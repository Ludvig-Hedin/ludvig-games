# The extremely unhelpful game's actually helpful solution

## Start

Click the plain word **HERE** under the green **NO** button. The obvious button and underlined words are decoys. Dismiss the fictional cookie banner. Hide support with **send to bottom**.

## 1. Account

Clear the actual prefilled values. Use a fictional split email such as `test` / `example` / `com`.

A valid fake password is `Sorry49!Яx`.

The rules are at least 10 characters, at least one uppercase ASCII letter, a digit, digits adding up to 13, the word `sorry` (case-insensitive), and a Cyrillic character. The Cyrillic helper appears when that rule is revealed. Uncheck **I do not accept**. Use the small gray **Next**, not **Cancel**.

## 2. Identity

Use any name of at least two characters. The optional name is not optional.

Use age **37**, country **Sweden**, and phone **555013**. The age slider is reversed: its internal value is 63 when the displayed age is 37. Watch the keypad rearrange after each number. Focus the phone display and type the digits for a keyboard alternative.

## 3. Preferences

Choose **Free**, not **FREE™ Plus**. Untick all three paid extras. The first extra will restore itself once; untick it again.

Select exactly three interests. Pink crossed-out tags are selected; green checked-looking tags are not. Five start selected, so remove any two selected tags, watching their rearrangement after the first click.

Turn **ON** the **Do NOT email me** switch. The fictional total must read **$0.00**. Click **Next**.

## 4. CAPTCHA

Use the checkbox above each model. Clicking a model rotates it.

Round 1: select **Donut**, **Roll** and **Ring**.

Round 2: select **nothing**. Clear the three selections carried over from round 1, then click Next. Invisible bicycles are not there. Refresh is cosmetic.

## 5. Terms

Scroll to the bottom. Dragging the scrollbar, using touch or focusing the text and pressing End bypasses the deliberately slowed mouse wheel.

The word in section 7 is **BANANA**. Enter it and check the agreement, then continue. The document updates once. Scroll to the new bottom, check the box again and continue. The word does not change.

## 6. Submission

Approach/tap the create button three times. It then stops moving. Tab and Enter, or reduced motion, also work.

In the confirmation, choose the small **Don't cancel** button.

When the progress stalls, click **99%** itself. You survived.

## Safety valve

Esc opens a real pause menu with stage-specific hints and skips. These are never traps. Hint penalties are 15 seconds; skips are 60 seconds. Skipped runs are marked assisted and do not replace unskipped local bests.

Information we use

Game data. Games save settings, progress, scores and sometimes drawings on your device. Shared features can store a random player ID and a generated or chosen nickname. There is no registered account. Online services are paused, but some games still send score requests containing your player ID, nickname and score to the hosting provider. Earlier leaderboard records may remain stored.

Technical data. Cloudflare hosts and protects the website and receives information such as your IP address, browser, requested page and request time. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits, referrers and page performance without analytics cookies or cross-site user tracking. Pages may load Google Fonts or libraries from CDNs such as jsDelivr, whose providers receive request information including your IP address.

Mac and nearby play. The app stores saves and room data on the host Mac. Local discovery advertises the computer name and app version. Player names and activity are shared with the room host and participating devices; the host can retain room data. The host temporarily tracks connected devices' local IPs, pages and activity. Local connections are not HTTPS encrypted. Google and Cloudflare STUN services may help establish connections. GitHub serves downloads and receives automatic update checks.

Support. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any information you provide. Please do not send passwords, card details or sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

We use data to provide requested game features and related support under our agreement with you. Our legitimate interests in delivering, securing and improving the service support technical requests, limited analytics, general enquiries and preserving earlier player records during the online-service pause. We may retain information to meet legal obligations or handle legal claims. We do not sell player data or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and retention

Browser and Mac saves have no automatic expiry. Clear the website's data in your browser to remove its saves. To remove Mac data, quit the app and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Ludvig Games/. This does not remove copies held by another room host. The host's connected-device list removes inactive entries after two minutes when checked.

Earlier cloud scores were pruned after new submissions, retaining a limited set of each player's best and recent runs; all-time records had no age limit. Pruning is paused with the online service. Those records are retained to preserve players' existing records during the pause. You can request deletion or object to that use by email. Support messages are retained until the enquiry and any related dispute are resolved, unless longer retention is legally required.

Providers retain technical and analytics data under their own policies for delivery, security, statistics and legal requirements. Those periods differ from local game saves.

Providers and international transfers

Data is shared with the providers above and with other players when you use shared play. It may also be disclosed where legally required or needed to handle a legal claim. Providers can process information outside the EU/EEA, including the US. Their published transfer arrangements include the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered certified entities and standard contractual clauses where applicable.

Read the privacy and transfer information from Cloudflare, Google, jsDelivr and GitHub. Contact us for information or copies of safeguards relevant to your data.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. We normally respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions, and may need information to identify your records. You can complain to IMY or another competent data-protection authority. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

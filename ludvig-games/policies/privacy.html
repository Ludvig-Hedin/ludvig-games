This policy covers games.ludvighedin.com and the Ludvig Games Mac app. It describes the current free service. Internet multiplayer and cloud leaderboards are paused. It does not cover unrelated apps or client websites linked from Ludvig's portfolio.

Game saves on your device

Games use browser storage for settings, progress, high scores and, in some games, creations such as saved drawings. Shared game features can save a random player identifier and a generated or chosen nickname. These are not a registered account.

Most local saves have no automatic expiry. They remain until you clear the website's stored data, the browser removes them, or the relevant game replaces them. A score-submission queue discards pending entries older than 24 hours when its script next runs; this does not delete your other saves.

To remove website saves, use your browser's site-data controls for games.ludvighedin.com. An in-game reset may not remove the shared player identifier. Local storage supports the game features and settings you use; blocking it can prevent saving.

Website requests and paused online features

Opening a page sends technical information such as your IP address, requested address, browser information and request time to the hosting infrastructure. Cloudflare delivers and protects the website and may process security and network-error reports.

Some games still attempt score requests while cloud features are paused. A submission can contain the game, random player identifier, nickname and score; leaderboard or records requests can include the player identifier. These requests reach the hosting provider even when the game API returns a not-found response. Paused online features therefore do not mean that all information stays on your device.

Scores submitted before the pause may remain in the historical leaderboard database, including a nickname, player identifier, score and timestamps. The earlier service did not set a universal time-based expiry for all records. Contact us if you want to ask about or delete an earlier submission; include your player identifier if you have it, without sending unrelated personal information.

Analytics, fonts and downloads

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page visits and performance, including page and referrer information, device or browser categories and loading measurements. Cloudflare describes this product as cookie-free and says it does not track individual users across its customers' websites. That does not mean there are no network requests or technical data. See Cloudflare's collection documentation and Privacy Policy.

Pages can load fonts from Google Fonts. Some games can load libraries or other resources from external content-delivery services such as jsDelivr. Those providers receive the technical information needed to answer the request, including your IP address and browser information. See Google's Privacy Policy and jsDelivr's Privacy Policy.

Mac downloads are served through GitHub Releases. The packaged Mac app checks GitHub for updates after startup and periodically while running; downloading an update contacts GitHub and its download infrastructure. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The Mac app and nearby players

The Mac app keeps game saves and local room databases in its application data on the host Mac. These do not have a universal automatic deletion deadline. Removing the app alone may leave its data behind. Its data folder is ~/Library/Application Support/Ludvig Games/. Quit the app before removing that folder if you want to delete all its local saves and room data.

Nearby discovery advertises the Mac's computer name, app version and temporary instance information on the local network. When you join a room, your nickname and gameplay information are shared with the participating devices and room host. The host can be another nearby Mac. Local room data may persist on that host, so deleting your own browser data does not delete another host's copy.

The host keeps a short-lived connected-device list with local IP addresses, device type, game, page path and last-seen time. Inactive entries are removed when the list is checked after two minutes. Connection discovery can also contact Google and Cloudflare STUN services. Local HTTP and WebSocket traffic is not encrypted by HTTPS; use a trusted network.

The local server removes the hub's Cloudflare analytics script. This is not a promise that every bundled game is entirely offline: fonts, libraries, connection discovery and update checks can still contact external services.

Purposes, legal grounds and retention

We process information needed to provide a feature you request or answer a related support request to perform our agreement with you or take steps at your request. Our legitimate interests in running a reliable, secure service and understanding its performance support technical delivery, abuse prevention, limited analytics and handling general enquiries. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Legal obligations can require records relating to a claim or transaction to be kept.

Local save durations are described above. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with the request and any related dispute, then deleted unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

The earlier leaderboard's retention rule was based on score ranking: after a new submission, it kept up to 30 all-time best runs, plus up to 10 best runs from each of the preceding 30-day and 7-day windows, per player and board. Lower-ranked runs outside those sets were deleted. All-time records therefore had no age limit. Because submissions and pruning are now paused, the earlier records can remain unchanged. They are held to preserve those player records during the pause, not for advertising. You can request erasure or object to that retention by email; records must be deleted when no longer needed for that purpose, unless another lawful retention ground applies.

For technical and analytics information processed by providers, retention is determined by the time needed to deliver the request, maintain service security, produce performance statistics and meet their legal obligations, as described in their linked retention policies. Those records do not inherit the local save or queue deadlines above. Contact us for details about information relating to your use of this service.

We do not sell player information or use it for targeted advertising. We share information with service providers as described here, other players when you use shared play, and authorities or advisers where legally required or necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

This page explains the refund policy for optional Ludvig Games digital extras when offered for sale. It does not mean those extras are already available to buy. It does not apply to unrelated apps or client projects.

14-day refunds

If you buy an optional Ludvig Games digital extra, you may request a full refund within 14 days after purchase, including if you have already tried or redeemed it. You do not need to give a reason. This voluntary policy does not limit any longer period or other remedy available under consumer law.

Contact ludvig@ludvighedin.com or the merchant named on your receipt. State that you want a refund and include your order reference or the email used for the purchase if available. Do not send your card details or password. You do not have to use special wording or a particular form to make a valid request.

Faulty, missing or wrongly described content

If paid content was not delivered, does not work as described, or you were charged incorrectly, contact us even after 14 days. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may be entitled to delivery, a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund. The 14-day voluntary period is not a deadline for exercising those rights.

Please include the game, order reference and a short description of the problem if you can. We will not make a statutory remedy conditional on using an extra first, providing a screenshot you cannot reasonably obtain, or accepting store credit instead of a refund you are entitled to.

Lemon Squeezy may also issue refunds under its own rules. Its discretionary refund practices do not shorten your legal rights. See its refund documentation.

Your legal right to withdraw

EU/EEA consumers generally have a 14-day right to withdraw from a distance purchase, with rules and exceptions depending on the product and how it is supplied. For digital content, loss of that statutory right requires the legally required express consent, acknowledgement and contract confirmation. Merely visiting this page, accepting general terms or using a pre-ticked box does not satisfy those requirements.

Our voluntary 14-day refund policy applies even after you try a digital extra. Nothing on this page waives statutory rights. Any online purchase flow must also provide the applicable withdrawal function and confirmation before sales are enabled.

You may use the Swedish Consumer Agency's withdrawal form, but it is optional. Send a withdrawal request to the merchant identified on the receipt; you can also email Ludvig for help.

## Applicable condition to resolve

Current [Konsumentverket business guidance](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/informationskrav-vid-distansavtal-regler-for-foretag/)
states that, since 19 June 2026, an online contract with a statutory withdrawal right must have an
accessible withdrawal function on the website/app where the contract was concluded. It must let the
consumer identify the contract and confirm how acknowledgement is delivered. The acknowledgement
must identify the withdrawn item and when the declaration was received, and arrive promptly.
Pre-contract information must identify the function. Contract confirmation must also supply the
required withdrawal information/form in a durable form where not already supplied that way.

The [consumer guidance](https://www.konsumentverket.se/lagar/lagen-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor-affarslokaler-konsument/)
distinguishes the online function from the option to send an email or letter. A mailto link alone
does not establish the required online-function journey. An acknowledgement records receipt of the
declaration; it is separate from deciding or executing the refund.

For these packs, establish the actual digital-content consent/waiver state rather than assuming
that every purchase has or lacks statutory withdrawal rights. The owner's separate, approved
14-day full-refund promise remains in force even after redemption. Do not replace it with a
waiver or no-refund policy to get through this check.

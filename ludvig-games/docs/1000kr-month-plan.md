# A plan for 1,000 kr a month

30 October 2026. Paid checkout is not live. Store inspection is complete; saving setup and test-mode
purchases are blocked by the installed read-only owner-browser policy. No end-to-end purchase test passed.
This is a sales experiment, not an income promise. No posting, spending or scheduler is activated by this plan.

## What 1,000 kr means

Aim for **1,000 kr monthly operating profit before personal income tax**, after fees, refunds and operating costs.
Customer spend, money earned, bank payouts and personal take-home are different numbers.
Count a fair allocated share of existing agent subscriptions in costs. Track owner/support time separately;
subtract an agreed value for that time when judging whether this is worth doing, not just cash-positive.

Approved one-time prices: Ritkaos+ **59 kr**, Pigeon pack **39 kr**, Karts pack **39 kr**, three-pack bundle **99 kr**.
Existing free content stays free. Exact new pack contents still need the concrete owner review.
No subscription, tiny individual extras or promise of every future release.

## How many orders we need

Reuse the [reviewed fee illustration](ai-games-business-research-2026-10-30.md#52-what-ludvig-keeps-per-sale-illustrative):
Swedish card buyer, 25% buyer VAT included, 6.5% + US$0.50/order, 1% payout fee, USD/SEK 9.8264.
Formula: `(price / 1.25 - 0.065 * price - 0.50 * 9.8264) * 0.99`.
This leaves about **67.17 kr per bundle** before our costs and income tax.
An assumed mix of 30% style packs, 30% Ritkaos+, 40% bundles leaves **45.34 kr/order**, from **69 kr customer spend**.
The mix, tax treatment and FX are assumptions, not measured demand or a payout quote.

All rows exclude refunds/chargebacks and personal tax. Costs are examples, not approved budgets.
With 300 kr costs, **35 mixed orders/month**, about eight a week, leave roughly **1,287 kr** before tax.
That gives only 287 kr of headroom above the goal, which refunds, changed fees or extra costs can consume.
First measure actual costs, then recalculate the target each month. Do not spend to match the example.
Lemon Squeezy has a **US$50 minimum payout** plus a holding/schedule process; earned money may remain pending.
These are one-time purchases: sustained months require new eligible buyers or separately approved new value.
We cannot charge existing owners again for the same pack.

## Four weeks, once the buyer journey works

**Before week 1, agent-owned:** finish pack previews, fulfilment/recovery/refund checks and cost-safe release.
Only expose Buy after every included entitlement works. Check existing analytics access and beacon coverage.
Prepare twelve short gameplay clips, with real footage, cleared audio and accurate paid/free descriptions.

**Week 1: show the fun.** Publish three approved clips, one per game, 10–20 seconds each.
Use these hooks: Pigeon stealing something ridiculous; a satisfying Karts drift; a Ritkaos wrong-guess reveal.
Show gameplay in the first second, then the actual free-play address available from that posting account.

**Week 2: show the extra.** Publish three clips using the other three hooks:
a Pigeon look in a funny moment; a Karts colour/design preview during a race; a Ritkaos Telephone reveal.
Show exactly what the paid pack adds. Do not market an unbuilt item, paused mode or unavailable game.
This completes the first six hooks, two per game. Record referrals and orders, not just video views.

**Week 3: repeat the best hook.** Make three new recordings of the hook that brings useful site traffic.
Inspect the first minute of play and purchase path. Fix confirmed failures before changing the offer.
Agent reports which clips led to visits and which product people bought. Attribution gaps remain explicit.

**Week 4: choose one next test.** Publish three more clips, then review qualified offer exposure, orders,
refunds, support time and costs. Continue the winning angle; do not add another channel, game or SKU by default.
If nothing brings visits, test a different opening or distribution route before cutting prices.

## Read the failure before adding work

- Views but few site visits: investigate the hook, call to action and actual account linking options.
- Visits but weak play: observe the first minute and repair friction; page views alone cannot prove play.
- People see the pack but do not buy: investigate its value and preview. Propose one offer change for approval.
- Checkout interest but failed orders: inspect payment and delivery; pause selling any broken entitlement.
- Refund/support cost exceeds contribution: stop expanding, repair the cause and preserve customer access/refund duties.

Four weeks is a review date, not a guaranteed sales deadline. Little exposure means insufficient evidence.
As a planning trigger, review the offer after 1,000 relevant extras-page views with few orders, but do not
treat repeated views as independent people or this threshold as a market benchmark.
Cloudflare Web Analytics supplies visits/page views by referrer definitions, not unique players or 7-day retention.
Use those limits honestly; any added measurement design needs its own scope/privacy review.
Weekly report: clips, referred visits, offer-page views, orders/product, customer spend, fees/tax, refunds,
operating costs, payout status and owner/support minutes. No fabricated traffic-to-sales funnel.

## What agents do, and what stays with Ludvig

Agents prepare clips/captions, research ideas, inspect permitted analytics, draft support and build approved work.
Tax information now shows **Submitted** in the store. Do not ask Ludvig to repeat that form.
Ludvig retains bank/identity/tax matters and reviews concrete pack/clip batches.
Target a 20–30 minute weekly review after setup stabilizes. Initial setup can take more; this is not guaranteed.
Posting requires a named account, approved batch and explicit posting limit. No permission to send messages,
run ads, enable paid tools, change platform accounts or create a scheduler is assumed.
Start organic with **0 kr new ad/tool spend**. Keep the existing server cost holds and Free-account plan.

## Should we make a new game?

**Not for this first experiment.** Keep the approved lineup and test whether players want its extras.
Next hypothesis, needing approval: a new **Karts track pack**, with current tracks staying free.
TrainWars' public offer bundles maps/tools at US$9.99 in page metadata; the friend's 1,000 kr week is
owner-reported, not verified profit. Study its clear paid value, not copy its maps, art or game.
If buyers ask for places to create/explore and existing games cannot serve that, pitch a bounded original
simulator prototype with one free location and optional paid environments. No new game is authorized here.

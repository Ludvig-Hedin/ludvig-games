## Checking real revenue

After the actual launch, a genuine customer can choose to buy the 39 SEK pack. Do not make an arranged
self-purchase or reimburse somebody to simulate revenue. The coordinator will inspect the live order,
receipt and licence delivery with the customer's consent, without exposing their key or card details.
The provider dashboard shows the order and net proceeds after applicable tax and fees.

A single 39 SEK sale will not normally produce an immediate bank payment. Lemon Squeezy's published
minimum payout is USD50. Lower balances roll over. It creates payouts twice monthly, holds net sales
for 13 days, and bank arrival can then take 1–5 days. The precise order/payout record is the evidence,
not a promise that a particular purchase date reaches the bank immediately.

# Roadmap · 23 October 2026

## Summary

- Multiplayer today: Ritkaos, Pigeon, Karts, Vibe Coder Tycoon and Anything Arena. Being built now: Horde co-op, Drop (optional). Next: Cubeville together, Higher or Lower duel, Dash and Roll live races.
- Keep polishing eight games that already feel good: Pigeon, Ritkaos, Flap, One Small Fake, No Brakes, Horde, Karts and Vibe Coder Tycoon.
- Every new feature runs on the existing Cloudflare Worker, D1 and one relay. Expected cost: 0 kr, at most about 55 kr a month on the Workers Paid plan.

## More multiplayer, ranked by fun per effort

| Game | Mode | Effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cubeville | Play obbies together, race to the top | Medium | It is a hangout game by nature |
| Higher or Lower | 1v1 duel, same pairs, fastest correct wins | Small | Cheap and very social |
| Dash, Roll | Live race on the same seed, see others as ghosts | Small | Same track, instant rivalry |
| Six Clues | Friends league, solve today's country first | Small | Daily ritual with friends |
| Horde | 2–4 player co-op against waves | Large (in progress) | Co-op shooters keep people playing |
| Drop | Up to 8 humans plus bots | Large (optional, in progress) | Real battle royale |
| Noodle | Real snakes instead of bots | Medium | The genre is built for multiplayer |
| Punchy | 1v1 online fights | Large | Needs careful netcode |
| Stacks | Versus with garbage lines | Medium | Classic head-to-head |
| Plunder | Fleet battles, 2–4 ships | Medium | Fits the sea map |
| Neon, Getaway | Shared city, cops vs robbers | Large | Fun, but heavy 3D on phones |

## Keep polishing (worth the time)

1. **Pigeon:** online rounds exist; add hats and unlocks, more quests and a second map.
2. **Ritkaos:** paid packs exist; next is a daily drawing, a gallery and a report button.
3. **Flap, Dash, Roll, Paper, Powder, No Brakes, One Small Fake:** daily challenges, unlocks, missions and ghosts (in progress).
4. **Horde:** co-op, smarter zombies, bosses and unlocks (in progress).
5. **Karts:** more tracks, online cups, better AI.
6. **Vibe Coder Tycoon:** longer progression and shared office events.

## Lower priority

Old prototypes and duplicate editions are hidden (originals are kept locally). Dust has no touch controls and stays desktop-only. Still House and Eight Notes are short horror pieces; a few more rooms would help.
